Welcome!

Jordyn Perkey is a creative writer who develops fictional narratives that accompany powerful digital art. Their art consists of developing characters within role-playing video games that seem larger than life, where the choices that you make affect the development of the character itself. Jordyn believes that art is always meant to invoke emotion, and that choices, no matter where we make them, represent our own moral code. These fictional narratives do not have a particular setting or character arc that is waiting to play out. Video games can give you freedom, but not without consequences. Regardless of the particular story, Jordyn develops dialogue choices, pathways, and minute interactions with characters that all have an unknown effect on the gaming experience. They believe that video games aren't meant to be an escape from reality, but in fact, they serve as a reflection of our reality through fiction and put the power back in our hands.
